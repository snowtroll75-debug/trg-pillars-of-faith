# Pillars of Faith 0.8.0 — Chapter 11, Magic Items

7 October 2026. Requires TRG Prime 3.626.0 (`release-notes-3.626.0.md` — what a module's magic items may declare). 565 documents across 19 packs: five new packs beside their core ones.

## What ships

- **pof-runes** (extends `runes`) — the fifteen runes as items, Requiem in three tiers (17). The behaviour is registered to the system (`module/magic-items.mjs`, 3.626.0's `registerRunes`), so each rune can be written into any armour, shield or weapon's runes field, chosen in Customise, and priced; the rune items are for the compendium and the hand.
- **pof-specific-items** (extends `specific-items`) — six armour and shields and seven weapons, each built on its Chapter 6 base with the printed quality, statistics and runes (Potency first, as the core writes it). The Hospice Vestment grants Light at will and Cure 3/day as innate spells and is the Administer First Aid kit; the Plate of the Sealed Sepulchre wears its essence resistance as an effect; Witness carries a Recall an Oath use.
- **pof-wondrous** (extends `wondrous`) — twenty wondrous items (the Stole of Divine Insight in four) and the two relics, in the core's own ability shapes: typed bonuses, innate spell grants (Final Toll's Last Rite, Seal the Word's Oathseal, Voice of the Dead's Dead Tongue, Read Vital Traces' Vital Diagnosis, Walk the Road's Long Stride, Open the Lantern's Veil Sight as a Focus use, the Thread's Anchor the Departing Soul), scoped offers (`conditionals`), ally auras (the Censer, the Lantern of the Last Road), a sense (the Lantern's Spirit Sense 60), a healing rider (Hands of Mercy's Steady Gift), and `use` abilities run by the module (Past-Life Insight, Perfect Reference, Borrowed Experience, Reveal the Unseen, the Lantern's Open the Last Road / Close the Gate).
- **pof-charged-items** (extends `charged-items`) — three rods (Intercession: +2 item on a Counteract for 2 PP; Merciful Channeling: +1d8 to one healed creature for 1 PP; Scholarly Recall: Advantage on its five actions for 2 PP) and five staves with their printed spell lists, PP and recharge, as staves (weapon form), with their held item bonuses as offers.
- **pof-consumables** (extends `consumables`) — twelve magical consumables: effects as spell rows (the Candle's and Waybread's scoped bonuses, Memory Spores' one-shot Advantage, the Resin's 3d8 for an Unliving creature only, the Vial's four-way choice, the Thread of Continuity's one-shot Advantage), the Soulguard Talisman as a worn save reroll, and the wards (Ashes, Grave Salt, Oath-Wax Seal, Incense) as cards with their printed rules.

## What is automated

Anchored Soul (+2 item pre-ticked on Soul/possession saves), Intercession (an ally's reroll offered from the save, 1/day), Last Vigil (relief offered after a Healing spell by its PP, 1/day), Sanctuary (Engage 1 AP: a minute's +2 status aura for allies and the wearer; Focus, Refocus restores it), Soul Ward (Soul Drain gained −5, −10 under Potency III, 1/minute), Veilwatch (Spirit Sense 30 feet on the senses), Banishing (2d6 essence and a Will ladder on a Critical against an Undead creature, once a round), Exorcist (strikes the incorporeal), Merciful (a felled creature left Stable, on offer), Remembrance (the Critical's card line and the +2 item offer), Requiem I–III (the tier's essence on the first hit each round against an Undead or Unliving creature), Soulchain (a Will ladder after a hit, once a round), Vowkeeper (the class bonus offered on damage while the vow is pursued). The wondrous items', rods', staves' and consumables' numbers as listed above.

## What is still the table's

Martyr's Aegis (transferring half an ally's damage), Soul Mirror (the possessor's save after your Critical save), Banishing's and Soulchain's Fumble clauses (a Counteract, a second save), the Vowkeeper's vow itself and whether it is kept, the Testament's once-a-day essence under the vow, the Plate of the Sealed Sepulchre's essence resistance origin, "disembodied spirit" and "possessing entity" as targets the system cannot see (the Undead creature type is what the gates read), the Lantern of the Veil's and Lantern of the Last Road's light and the outline of spirits within it, the Ashen Censer's outline, the Censer of Quiet Thresholds' "while incense burns" (the aura is carried whenever the censer is on the sheet), Pilgrim's Waystone's direction, the Archivist's Concordance as a Prayer Book, the Tablet's reading of any sacred language, the Thread of the Returning Soul's death trigger (the spell is held to cast then), the Candle's "until your next Full Rest" (written for 24 hours), Consecrated Grave Salt's and Ashes of Final Passage's wards (cards with the ladder and the Counteract level), the Oath-Wax Seal.

## Harness

`verify-magic-items.mjs` — 591 checks: every count, every level and price against the chapter's text, each rune's traits and usage, each specific item's printed statistics and traits line, each wondrous ability in a shape the system reads (its use registered, its spell shipped), each charged item's spells and PP, each consumable's effect — and the declarations driven through TRG Prime's own readers (the catalogue, the senses, the strike riders, the item healing riders, the rods, the consumable choice, the save rerolls). Seven suites in `npm run verify`; foundation grew to 757.
