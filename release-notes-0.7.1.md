# Pillars of Faith 0.7.1 — after the live test

7 October 2026. Requires TRG Prime 3.625.1 (the fixes the live test of 0.7.0 asked for — `release-notes-3.625.1.md`). Live-test report: `pillars-of-faith-live-testing-report-0.7.0.md`.

- **Share Burden** finishes itself: the caster takes the relieved condition on, one step higher, on the card; an immunity refuses it (`casterGains`, 3.625.1). The "set it on your sheet" note is gone.
- **Swift Mender** and **Guiding Luminescence** are checkboxes in the Warp? dialog rather than "× how many times" counts (`once`); so is Saintly Intercession's +2 status warp.
- The three defects the test found — Consecrated Flame firing every round, Dragon's Breath Reed spent on a miss, Vital Surge once a day out of combat — were in the system and are fixed there; this module's data was right.

Harness: verify-automation 51, grimoire 2,080; the rest unchanged (foundation 742, classes 897, feats 3,030, equipment 1,330).
