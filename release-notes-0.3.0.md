# Pillars of Faith 0.3.0 — Chapter 3, the five classes

17 September 2026. Built against TRG Prime 3.563.0 (required — see its release notes for the four generic mechanisms this block leans on).

## What ships

**Five class items** (pack `pof-classes`, extends `classes`) in exactly the shape the core eight carry: Archivist, Folkwarden, Grimward, Healer, Shaman. Each carries its twenty-row table (extracted from the book text and diffed in the harness against the column arithmetic and the first and last printed rows), key ability scores, class skills, HP and skill points per level, the printed proficiency sentence, its granted feats, its levelled `classAbilities`, magical tradition, ability and caster style, cantrip counts, the Chapter 6 Class Wealth value (Archivist 1, Folkwarden 0, Grimward 1, Healer 1, Shaman −1) and the printed magical-tradition and bonus-feat notes. The Archivist, Healer and Shaman carry the "+1 Control Level per level" as an effect on the field the cap already reads.

**Sixty-two class abilities** (in `pof-abilities`, `sourceClass` set, `classAbility {class, level}` flag as the core ones): every printed ability, with the book's paragraph as its description and a line saying what the sheet does with it. Handed over by the class's levelled grant as the character reaches 2nd, 3rd, 4th and 7th where the book says so.

**The five sub-choices as choosers.** Archival Discipline, Folk Tradition, Inquisitorial Discipline, Healer's Calling and Spirit Guide each declare `abilityChoices`, and the system's new generic chooser offers the five and grants the chosen ability. Each option carries its own specialist skill (`grantedSpecialistSkills` — Canon Lore, Burial Lore, Chirurgery, Veil Lore…; the Land Spirit asks for its Terrain Lore), its specialist spells (the Archivist's ten, levelled 1 and 7) and its scoped bonuses as conditionals. Choosing is one Use; the option leaves with the chooser.

**Casting.** The Archivist is a learned book caster: `memorisationStyle: book`, six PP 2 Divinity spells at 1st and two a level, four cantrips a day, memorising 2 + level × 2 — the Wizard's machinery with a Divinity tradition, and an **Archivist's Prayer Book** gear item (pack `pof-gear`, a spellbook container) granted by the class. The Folkwarden is an instinctive Verdant caster on the core schedule with two cantrips known. The Grimward, Healer and Shaman declare their printed repertoire schedules (one pick a level from 2nd; the Healer and Shaman one swap a level from 5th) through the new class flag. The Healer's Cure, Restoration and Stabilise are granted by the class outside its repertoire count, plus Touch of Healing as its 1st-level specialist spell.

**Registered:** the five classes (proficiency profile in the system's own shape — the Grimward's counts its deity's favoured weapon; the Grimward alone needs a Divine Aspect, so the sheet's slot opens for it); Spirit Speaker's Nature-for-Occultism substitution on Trace Possession and Interpret Haunt; Seasoned Hands' Nature-for-Medicine on Treat Disease and Treat Toxin and its fumble → failure on Read the Signs, Lay to Rest, Treat Disease, Treat Toxin and Treat Wounds; Read the Signs (Nature/Survival, GM's DC) and Lay to Rest (Medicine/Nature/Religion vs Level DC) as skill actions offered only to their owner.

**Nineteen Use activations** (module/class-automations.mjs): Recite from the Text (Focus; marks an unmemorised book spell prepared); Authoritative Interpretation (+1 status to saves on self or targeted ally until your next turn); Calculated Portent (daily save choice, then the Reaction); Warding Charm (Mind/Soul/Hex, two for a Charmwright — an effect on the carrier with the bonus pre-ticked on matching saves), Brew Remedy (Disease/Poison, two doses for Herbal), Ward the Threshold (every targeted creature and you; 20 ft for a Hearthkeeper; Use again to end) — each counting its Preparation against the class bonus; Inquisition (1 AP; targeted token or typed name; the class bonus offered on the seven investigation actions and Sense Motive); Judgement (+1 circumstance to attacks against and saves from the Subject, pre-ticked by uuid when the Subject was a token; the card states 1d6/2d6/3d6 secondary damage and Sanctioned Strike from 4th; Grave Warden's essence note); Prepared Adversary (+10 class to Initiative for the roll); Gift of Healing (level + Wis to the targeted recipient, Restorative Overflow's temp HP from 3rd, Sanctuary Keeper's +2 status DB, Lifebinder's reminder); Healer's Touch (Focus Reaction; heals every target by the PP entered); Restorative Hands (reduces the PP-band condition on the target; Soul Tender's +5); Lifeline (Focus Reaction; 1 HP, no Dying, Fatigued 1); Spirit Communion (picks the day's Ethereal spell(s) — 1/2/3/4 to PP 2/4/10/14 — from the federated spells pack, clearing yesterday's); Manifest Spirit (Focus; the guide's one-minute effect — Chorus of the Past, Beast Within, Elemental Mantle, Rooted World, Part the Veil — Use again to dismiss); Spirit Intercession (Focus Reaction; allies from 7th); Elemental Spirit (element choice, +1 class vs that trait); Land Spirit (Terrain Lore); Ancestor Spirit (Borrowed Memory's daily use).

**Passive and rules-for-the-table**, each saying so on its sheet hint: Scholarly Exegesis, Practical Counsel, Reconstruct Target, Sanctioned Strike, Deepened Bond, Lifebinder's Vital Surge, Restorative Overflow's arithmetic (done by Gift of Healing's Use), Beast Within's Scent, Part the Veil's targeting, Hearthkeeper's room-shaped ward.

## Rulings needed from Graham

1. **Healer's magical ability score.** The book says "Charisma is your magical ability score. You add your Wisdom modifier to your Spellcraft bonus and as a static bonus to your Power Points." — a contradiction. The key ability line is Wisdom (min +2) and the operative sentence says Wisdom, so the class item uses **Wisdom**. One field to change if Charisma was meant.
2. **The specialist spells** the disciplines and the Healer grant — Doctrinal Ward, Word of Censure, Hidden Doctrine, Forbidden Name, Last Rite, Anchor the Departing Soul, Exemplar's Grace, Saintly Intercession, Read Omen, Unfold the Future, Touch of Healing — are Chapter 10 and ship with the Grimoire block. Until then the Apply Grants card names them as unresolved (the system's loud-not-silent rule). The Folkwarden's and Shaman's "one PP 1 specialist spell of your choice" likewise wait for the Grimoire's list; nothing on the class asks for it yet.
3. **Judgement's secondary damage** is stated on the card, not added to the damage roll — the attack workflow has no rider hook a module can reach. If it should be automatic, that is a system mechanism (the Ranger's Precision Hunter has the shape) to generalise.
4. **Inquisition's bonus** is authored at Use time from the current class bonus and offered (unticked) on the seven actions; a level-up needs a re-Use. A live value expression in conditionals would remove that step.
