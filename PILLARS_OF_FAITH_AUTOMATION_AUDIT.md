## What changed since the 0.12.0 audit (0.12.1)

Graham's review: "you have definitely missed things. Elemental Spirit for example is not granting a free cantrip." He was right, and the cause was in the audit tooling as well as the module.

- **The 0.12.0 classifier never saw most sheet lines.** The inventory kept only the first 600 characters of each description. On a long entry, the module's own "Works automatically… / On the sheet…" line comes last and was cut off. So "note the +15 ft by hand" or "the 30-foot light is the table's" never reached the check that marks an entry C. The inventory now keeps the whole description and pulls out the sheet line separately. Classification reads that line.
- **The manual-step scan is broader.** Any sheet line that leaves a step to the table, the GM, a note or the player's hand now makes the entry C, even when the rest is automated. That includes "the table's", "a rule for the table", "is a note", "by hand", "the GM's", "from your sheet" and "GM to apply".
- **Fixed in 0.12.1 (F11–F16):**
  - Elemental Spirit's cantrip;
  - Second Discipline's withheld 7th-level spell;
  - Sacred Marginalia's four Prayer Book spells;
  - the Liturgist's book, daily selection and Liturgy of Prayer;
  - the two lanterns' light and the relic's essence Resistance;
  - Final Toll's ladder.
  
  The five rulings of 16 November are also in. See `PILLARS_OF_FAITH_REPAIR_REPORT.md`.

The C count rose from 21 to 86 at 0.12.1 (85 at 0.12.2). Nothing got worse. The 0.12.0 audit under-reported it. Every C entry is listed with its manual step in the matrix and in `PILLARS_OF_FAITH_UNRESOLVED.md`.

## How this was audited (0.12.0 method, still applies)

Every entry in all 22 compendiums was inventoried from `packs-source/` (661 documents; the builders in `tools/content/` reproduce that tree byte for byte, so it is the module's real content). For each entry the matrix records:

- the rules text (the book's text the module carries);
- every automation mechanism found on it — Active Effect changes, the declarations TRG Prime reads (`conditionals`, `consumable`, `rune`, `auras`, grants, spell rows…), and the Use handlers, passive hints and registry entries the module registers by name;
- the module's own "Works automatically / Use / On the sheet" line, which states what is meant to happen;
- what was done to verify it.

Machine checks then looked for false automation:

- AE change keys checked against the actor schema in `template.json`;
- every core helper, `register…` call and hook the module uses checked against 3.694.0. No API drift: all 22 register calls and every helper exist, and every hook the module listens to is still fired;
- entries with mechanical text but no mechanism;
- entries whose only evidence is a tooltip;
- direct writes to creatures the client may not own.

**Live tests** were then run in Graham's world as GM: five full 1–20 class progressions, a sweep driving 93 Use automations, 70 herbs and consumables, equip/unequip, the Undead Lords option and the repairs. See `PILLARS_OF_FAITH_TEST_REPORT.md`.

**0.12.1 live drive.** The new Liturgist, Liturgy of Prayer, Sacred Marginalia and lantern-light code, and Final Toll's two core calls, were run in the world against temporary copies of the QA characters. The copies were deleted afterwards. See `PILLARS_OF_FAITH_TEST_REPORT.md`.
