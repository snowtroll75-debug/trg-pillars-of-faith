# Pillars of Faith 0.12.3: Eternal Resonance fixed, on TRG Prime 3.695.0

16 November 2026. **Requires TRG Prime 3.695.0.** Install the core first. Document ids are unchanged.

## Eternal Resonance

- **What went wrong.** The live test of 0.12.2 showed Eternal Resonance doing nothing: an innate Chill Touch's Save DC had no +1. 0.12.2 put the bonus in a casting option, and the core never offers casting options to an innate cast.
- **The fix.** The feat now declares the bonus on its own effect, +1 racial to the spell attack and the Save DC of every innate spell, an item's included. TRG Prime 3.695.0 reads that on every cast; it adds the spell-attack half (CD14).
- **Existing characters.** The feat's effect comes from the compendium, so a character who already has Eternal Resonance needs it re-added, or the compendium refresh run, to pick up the new effect.

## Mummy Rot

TRG Prime 3.695.0 gives Remove Disease its counteract. Before, it did nothing on the sheet. Remove Curse also now reaches curse afflictions. Both follow Mummy Rot's order:
- Remove Curse comes first;
- Remove Disease follows;
- the true results are whispered to the GM until the cure is complete.

0.12.3 declares that order where the core reads it. Cleansing Radiance records its steps in the same place as the core, so the two can be mixed.

## Also confirmed live on 0.12.2

Cleansing Radiance ended Frightened and Blinded, and cured Mummy Rot only in the right order.
