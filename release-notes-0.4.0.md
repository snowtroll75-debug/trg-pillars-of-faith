# Pillars of Faith 0.4.0 — Chapter 5, Feats

18 September 2026. Built against TRG Prime 3.571.0 (required — this block leans on registered feat access, weapon grants from feats, the module class-skill chooser and modifier ally auras, all new in 3.571.0; Graham's 3.570.1 changes are underneath).

## What ships

**179 feats in pof-feats** — the twelve background feats of 0.1.0 and Chapter 5's four families: 93 racial (the Morrowmade and Vesperyn heritage feats and their trees, the five Eternal Blood heritages and their follow-ups, and the Rhevani, Eshari, Kethrani, Myrathi and core-race feats), 13 archetypes, 48 class feats (Archivist, Folkwarden, Grimward, Healer, Shaman, and the Adept/Expert/Martial tiers) and the 13 general feats not already shipped. Every item carries the book's trait line, body, prerequisites and progression requirements, then one sentence on what the sheet does.

**Six archetype abilities in pof-abilities** — Inquisition (Inquisitor), Liturgy of Prayer, Share the Wound, Vowbound Resolve, Applied Insight, Ward the Soul — granted by their archetype feats; the **Liturgical Book** (a spellbook container) in pof-gear from the Liturgist; and a new **pof-companions** pack extending the system's companions pack with the eight natural attacks feats hand over (Fangs for Hunter's Fangs; Bite, Claw, Bone Spur and Heavy Appendage for Grafted Armament; three Predator's Manifestation attacks).

**One skill action** — Read the Room, from Sense Motive, gated on its feat.

## How the feats work

Everything a feat does unconditionally is data on the item: typed bonuses as effect changes on pool slots (Base DR, saves, Speed, resistances, immunities, traits), conditional bonuses and advantage as declared conditionals (pre-ticked where a trait or action settles it, offered with the book's wording where the table decides), senses on the creature-ability sense derivation (Blood Sense, Spirit Sense at 15 or 30 feet), innate Focus spells and abilities as grants, Lore skills as specialist-skill grants, natural attacks as weapon grants, and the four aura feats (Keeper at the Threshold, Lantern of the Last Road, Lantern of the Bloom, Domain of the Land) as ally auras. The archetypes register: the five class-access ones (Inquisitor → Grimward Expert, Lifebearer → Healer Adept, Liturgist → Archivist Adept, Spirit Walker → Shaman Adept, Hedge Witch) open those class-feat lists; Inquisitor and Lifebearer offer their class-skill choice on Use through the system's chooser. Three feats open actions from another skill (Fey-Soul Listener, Lay Exorcist, Bloom-Tender's Spores) and three upgrade a degree (Close Reading, Expert Autopsist, Watch the Eyes).

**Sixty Use automations** in `module/feat-automations.mjs`: the Focus feats (Adaptive Tissue's chosen resistance, Perfected Aberration, Blood Awakened, Continuity Unbroken, Not Yet Finished, Share the Wound, Vowbound Resolve, Applied Insight's Opening or Warning, Ward the Soul...), the daily choices (Prayer from Another Life, Remembered Liturgy and Living Gospel fetch the day's Divinity spell from the federated spells pack and land it innate with Focus; Whisper Network, Many Roads Many Gods, Oathkeeper's vow, Field Practice, Old Secret, Prophet's Portent), the reactions and after-the-roll queues (Death Chant with Chorus of the Fallen, Toll of Passage, Mercy Written Deep, Hauntwise, Lay Exorcist, Watch the Eyes, Measured Invocation, Lifebound Ward, Restorative Spores), the marks (Consequence Written; Sentence of Judgement and Eternal Manifestation as strike damage riders), Grafted Armament's chosen attack from the pack, Votary's Aspect spell read off the owned Divine Aspect's specialist list, the Inquisitor's own Inquisition, Liturgy of Prayer, Twin-aware Expose the Subject, Shared Manifestation, Methodical Investigator's chosen action. **113 passive hints** say what the sheet already does for the rest.

## Readings and things to flag

- **Mesmeric Gaze** prints "Velvet Gaze" as a prerequisite; no such feat exists. Shipped as **Velvet Voice** (typo assumed).
- **The Testament Stands Between** ends mid-sentence in the book ("Resistance to the triggering"). Shipped as Resistance to the triggering attack's damage type, chosen at Use.
- **Veil Warden**'s Spirit Sense prints no range; 30 feet assumed.
- **Eternal Manifestation** prints no frequency; it is a 1 AP toggle with no daily use.
- **Read the Room** collides with a core racial feat of the same name; the class feat is **Read the Room (Expert)** on the sheet (the grant dedupe and the action gate are both by name).
- **Veil Tracker** and **Ancestral Stone**'s "use this specialist skill for that action" cannot be a substitution (specialist skills carry no actions); their bonuses are offered instead.
- **Ancestral Chorus** upgrades only the check under Ancestral Communion, so it is a card line rather than a degree upgrade.
- Feats whose effect the roll cannot see (Final Sanction's half-HP die, Consecrated Flame, Elemental Overflow, Resurgent Healing, No Life Abandoned...) are notes on the card and the sheet.
- The Prophet's Read Omen and Old Secret's borrowed spells wait for the Grimoire block, as the class specialist spells do.
