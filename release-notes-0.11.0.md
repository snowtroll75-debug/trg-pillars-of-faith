# Pillars of Faith 0.11.0 — the automation pass, and Undead Lords

8 October 2026. Requires TRG Prime 3.630.0.

Graham asked for two things. The first was the rules that were still card-only, automated wherever the system could carry them, built from the classification in *pillars-of-faith-automation-pass.md* and his rulings of 8 October. The second was the Undead Lords bonus Archetype feat as a checkbox.

## Undead Lords campaign (new world setting)
**Configure Settings → Pillars of Faith → Undead Lords campaign.** When ticked:
- every player character of 2nd level or higher gets a **Bonus Archetype** pick, shown in the feat banner as *Bonus Archetype: x / 1*;
- their first Archetype feat (regular or Unliving) spends it rather than a Class pick, and later ones draw on Class picks as usual;
- prerequisites are the feat's own, so Unliving Lich (10th level) still waits.

Changing the box redraws open sheets.

## Runes, class abilities and feats
- **Martyr's Aegis:** when an ally within 10 feet is about to take HP damage, a wearer is offered up to half of it.
  - The prompt goes to whoever applies the damage, usually the GM.
  - The wearer can't go below 1 HP, it costs a Reaction, and it works once a minute.
- **Soul Mirror:** after a Critical or Heroic save against a possession effect, the possessor rolls Will against your Level DC and takes Frightened 1–3. If the roll doesn't know the possessor, the card asks the GM.
- **Restless End (Grave Warden):** +1d6 on Judgement damage against Undead or Unliving, added to the damage roll. It assumes the Essence choice.
- **Memory of Lost Masters:** a Critical or Heroic on the remembered Lore's first check gives Past-Life Echo's use back.
- **Elemental Mantle:** its secondary damage now rolls on the spell's card, once a round, on Verdant spells of your element.
- **Elemental Overflow:** when the Mantle's damage lands, you pick a second creature within 10 feet. It takes Con + half your Shaman level, once a round per creature.
- **Veil Tracker:** rolling Spirit Lore from the sheet offers Track.
- **Grasp the Unseen:** your Strikes are Ghost Touch while Shadow Stance is up.
- **Living Concordance:** Spell Substitution asks for the higher DC of two spells, and its card says both are swapped.
- **Conceal Faith:** the check's total becomes "Conceal Faith — DC N" on your effects.
- **Perform Last Rites:**
  - A Success leaves "+2 Casting DC to possess, animate or bind" on the remains, for 24 hours, 7 days or a month depending on the roll and on Guide the Last Road.
  - A Fumble marks the remains, and further Last Rites on them are refused until the GM deletes the mark.
